Education OS

not just a prototype, live in production!

Education OS is a live, multi-tenant generic LMS education platform focused on music schools. It brings learning materials, assignments, feedback, and live teaching into one protected environment while allowing each institution to retain its own curriculum, workflows, branding, and permissions.

The Coach Can

Build Their Own Library

  • Create courses & lessons: Upload backing tracks, sheet music, or movement drills.
  • Private school access: Keep all learning materials scoped strictly to approved school members.
  • Attach to homework: Link library items directly onto student assignments.

Assign Homework

  • Set structured practice: Assign tasks to individuals or groups between lessons.
  • Review private uploads: Inspect student audio or video submissions safely.
  • Timestamped & visual feedback: Leave time-coded notes or draw directly on video frames.

Check the Attendance

  • Approve members: Review and accept invited students into the school workspace.
  • Live attendance log: Check who attended scheduled classes and track history.
  • Student progress view: See active members, pending invites, and completed work.

Conduct Live Class

  • Built-in live rooms: Host Daily.co classes inside the school without external links.
  • Dual-channel audio: Separate voice mic from instrument or backing-track audio.
  • In-call student context: View the student's homework and past recordings while teaching.

The Student Can

Submit Homework & Get Feedback

  • Review assigned tasks: Open structured instructions and attached reference tracks between lessons.
  • Upload practice recordings: Submit private audio takes or training footage directly to the assignment.
  • Revisit coach feedback: Read timestamped notes and watch visual video drawings anytime.

Track Personal Practice

  • Training log: Log daily practice sessions and track personal progress over time.
  • Personal uploads: Store private practice audio takes and training videos in one protected place.
  • Recorded sessions: Replay past live lessons and coach demonstrations whenever needed.

Join Live Classes

  • Real-time live room: Enter classes inside the school with clear voice and music audio channels.
  • In-call lesson context: Keep active homework, reference tracks, and coach notes open during the call.
  • No external links: Transition straight from practice materials into the live lesson.

Explore Library & Community

  • Learning library: Browse the school's structured courses, sheet music, and technique breakdowns.
  • Direct coach messaging: Ask questions and stay connected with the coach between classes.
  • School community feed: Participate in shared updates and discussions inside the school workspace.
where the whole project started

It began with a problem I experienced personally while studying music on-line and offline.

Between lessons, sharing sheet music, backing tracks, practice recordings, and coach feedback was scattered across chat threads, cloud links, and separate call tools. total workflow chaos! I could not find a platform built for this ongoing learner-coach loop, so I built one.

Project Timeline

Eight months of iterative design and implementation.

Month 01 · 8 Months Ago
Feb 2026

Personal Music Problem

Started from personal friction experienced between music lessons while trying to keep practice and coach feedback in one place.

  • Audit of fragmented workflows across chat threads, cloud links and voice notes.
  • Core product hypothesis uniting references, practice, recordings and feedback.
  • First relational data model for ongoing learner-coach continuity.
Months 02-03 · Stage 1
Mar - Apr 2026

Melodina Hub

Built and launched the first working application focused on music coaching and longitudinal practice history.

  • Connected music workspace linking lesson references and assignments.
  • Private practice audio uploads with persistent coach feedback threads.
  • Live classroom exploration separating voice mic from accompaniment audio.
  • Active onboarding of a real music coach and learners for longitudinal use.
Months 04-05 · Stage 2
May - Jun 2026

Mira Treino

Adapted the coach-led model to martial arts, proving the relationship loop held while media workflows diverged.

  • Training-video submission pipeline for physical movement and sparring drills.
  • Time-coded coach review pinned to exact timestamps in movement footage.
  • On-video telestration & drawing for visual posture and form annotation.
  • Key architectural insight: shared coaching loop vs. discipline-specific modules.
Months 06-07 · Stage 3
Jul - Aug 2026

Education OS

Unified Melodina Hub and Mira Treino as isolated tenants on a single multi-tenant React, TypeScript and Supabase architecture.

  • Separation of identity vs. access via tenant membership and educator approval.
  • Row Level Security (RLS) and short-lived signed URLs for private learner media.
  • Per-tenant configuration for branding, curriculum structure and enabled modules.
  • Separation of platform admin from school-level educator authority.
Month 08 · Current Live Phase
Sep - Oct 2026

Strengthen & Validate

Hardening multi-tenant edge cases, permission synchronization and white-label administration in active production.

  • Fail-closed tenant switching preventing cross-school UI or control leakage.
  • Direct-link context preservation returning users to deep routes after sign-in.
  • Explicit onboarding states for pending, missing or unapproved memberships.
  • Ongoing longitudinal testing with live educators and students.
Next Phase · Future Direction
Q4 2026+

Additional Niche Pilots

Validating the shared multi-tenant foundation across new specialised coaching communities without generic LMS compromises.

  • Self-serve white-label provisioning for new niche schools and academies.
  • Documented longitudinal evidence from Melodina Hub & Mira Treino cohorts.

Closed-Loop Continuity

Designer to Product Builder

Education OS became an experiment in how far I could extend my practice as a product designer using AI-assisted development. My working loop became:

Define the workflow → implement it → exercise it in the browser → inspect unexpected behaviour → revise the product logic → test again

Even though I didn’t code it from scratch, I architected the integration. After AI built the platform based on my design, I had to reverse-engineer the logic to master troubleshooting.

  • Frontend: React & TypeScript
  • Backend & Data: Supabase (Auth, Postgres, RLS, Storage)
  • Live & Media: Daily.co & Bunny
